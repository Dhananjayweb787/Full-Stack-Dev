Level-1: The Interactive Mystry Case File

Difficulty:4/10

The Conecpt

Build a Website that presents a fictional unsloved mystry, might also take from a real case too(caution: Be factual if real).

Imagine a dectective has given you acess to a digital case file. Your job is to organize all the evidence so another investigator can solve the case.

Try your best to make it feel like a tasty site, not a webpage. The truth I want you to know is that, if you only use html, then your site is just going to be dull. Don't worry, we are going to change it in CSS.

Your Mission

Create or get a case file about an incident.Then, your Site must contain:

  1. Case Overview
    • Case number and status
    • Incident date and loaction
    • A short incident summary
  2. Suspect prfiles
    • Suspects name,profile,address,occupations and thier statments if any
    • Thier possible motives
    • A short summary for where they were at the time of crime
  3. Evidence archive
    • A timeline of events
    • Photographs or illustrations or evidence
    • An evidence table containing timestamps,descriptions, and reliability ratings
  4. Witness statements
    • Statments presented using appropriate quotation elements
    • Contradictions between different witnness
  5. investigator's notes
    • Important observations
    • Unanswered questions
    • A final theory about what might happened
Completion test:Can another person understand the case analisies wihtout your interventions.
